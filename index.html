Selected Projects
Final Year Project IEEE Published

RxLense

A multi-stage transformer pipeline that reads handwritten medical prescriptions and explains them in patient-friendly language — the first system of its kind grounded in Sri Lankan clinical conventions, with a drug-verification layer against the NMRA national registry. A fine-tuned YOLO11 model detects prescription layout, TrOCR transcribes the handwriting (halving the character error rate of zero-shot baselines), BioBERT extracts drugs, dosages, frequencies and durations, and generative AI produces safe, plain-language explanations — all deployed end-to-end in a Flutter mobile app. Every model was trained on domain-specific datasets built entirely from scratch, validated with 5-fold cross-validation and OCR-noise robustness testing.
